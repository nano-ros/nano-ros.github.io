## Overview

Tier 4 targets defense and aerospace platforms where SpaceWire is not available but MIL-STD-1553B is mandated.

## Basics

MIL-STD-1553B is a 1 Mb/s differential serial bus with a time division multiplexing architecture.

## Wire format

T4 carries the same CCSDS framing as the space tier, packed into 1553B data words.

## Callback contract

The interrupt marks message receipt complete and wakes the transport task. No callback runs from ISR context.

## Use cases

- Defense ground robots
- Legacy aerospace programs
- Platforms where 1553B is required
